The AgentPass Pro plan allows you to make decisions on crawlers, such as allow, charge, or block, and sell paid keys. Enterprise plan offers every module, including Private Access Tokens for secure access. Check the plans: https://attestos-edge.attestos.workers.dev/go/pricing?src=c-promo-plans-publisher
