# Every bot token should work exactly once

channel: tumblr
kind: value

Abuse on store forms can be costly. Card testing at checkout, where bots attempt to validate stolen cards, can lead to chargeback fees. Credential stuffing on login forms can compromise customer accounts. Fake accounts and promo farming can drain your marketing budget. Gift-card guessing can result in financial losses. Visible puzzles, meant to stop bots, can also deter real buyers, increasing cart abandonment rates. A better approach is to use single-use tokens, which expire quickly, making it difficult for bots to replay them. Check your form logs for replay attempts, test your form with a single token twice to see if it's accepted, and consider a defence that doesn't rely on visible puzzles. Single-use tokens can help prevent abuse without frustrating customers.
