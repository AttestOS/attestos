# Headless browsers announce themselves more than they think

channel: tumblr
kind: value

Headless browsers can announce themselves in several ways, making it easier to detect automation. Webdriver flags and headless user agents are two common signals, as they often identify themselves as such. Software graphics renderers and missing plugins can also be indicative of a headless browser, as they may not have the same capabilities as a traditional browser. These signals can be used to build a risk score, helping to identify potentially malicious traffic. By analyzing these signals, online store owners can better protect their sites from abuse, such as card testing, credential stuffing, and fake account creation, without relying on visible puzzles that can frustrate real buyers.
