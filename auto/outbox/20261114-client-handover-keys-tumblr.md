When building and maintaining client sites, it's essential to consider the security and accessibility of contact forms. Server-side verification on every form can help prevent spam, but it's crucial to handle the keys and secrets properly. Create keys under the client's account, not your own, to ensure they have control over their site's security. Document where the secret lives, so it's easily accessible when needed. Rotate keys on handover to prevent unauthorized access. Domain allowlists can also help reduce spam. Monitoring verify failures and having a spam incident response plan in place can help mitigate potential issues. By prioritizing security and accessibility, you can provide better services to your clients and protect their sites from unwanted spam.
