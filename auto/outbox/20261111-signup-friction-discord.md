# Every visible check on a signup form costs signups

channel: discord
kind: value

When a visitor decides to try your SaaS, they're met with a test: a puzzle on the signup form. This visible check costs you signups, particularly from those who need accessibility features like screen readers. Invisible checks, on the other hand, keep the cost on automation. Server-side token verification can be done right with single-use tokens, expiry, and informative error codes. Proof of work raises the cost of each attempt for attackers. Risk scores can be used to fork traffic, and passkeys with adaptive difficulty provide an additional layer of security. Check your signup form's friction and consider alternatives that don't deter genuine users.
