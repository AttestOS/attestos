# Every visible check on a signup form costs signups

channel: discord
kind: value

When a visitor decides to try your SaaS, they're met with a test: a puzzle on the signup form. This visible check comes at a cost, paid for by the people you most want - potential customers. Puzzles can fail screen reader users, creating accessibility issues. Invisible checks, like proof of work, keep the cost on automation, not users. Server-side token verification can be done right with single use, expiry, and error codes. Risk scores can be used as a fork, directing suspicious traffic to additional checks. Passkeys and adaptive difficulty can also help. Consider the cost of each visible check on your signup form.
