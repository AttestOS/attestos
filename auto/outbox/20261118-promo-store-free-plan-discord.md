# An invisible check for small stores

channel: discord
kind: promo

Abuse on store forms is a real issue: card testing, credential stuffing, fake accounts, and promo farming. A visible puzzle can drive away real buyers. AttestOS offers a free plan with 10,000 verifications a month on 1 site, no puzzle for shoppers. Proof of work runs in the background, one server call, same shape as reCAPTCHA's.
