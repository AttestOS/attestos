# Scan your signup page

channel: tumblr
kind: promo

Signup and free-trial abuse can be a major issue for SaaS products. API key farming and server-side token verification are common targets. To combat this, implementing proof of work, risk scores, and passkeys can be effective. Proof of work raises the cost of each attempt, making it more difficult for attackers. Risk scores can be used to flag suspicious activity, and passkeys provide an additional layer of security. Adaptive difficulty can also be used to adjust the level of security based on the user's behavior. Check your signup page's security with the free scanner: https://attestos-edge.attestos.workers.dev/go/scanner?src=s-promo-saas-scan
