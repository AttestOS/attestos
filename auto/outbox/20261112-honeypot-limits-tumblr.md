# Honeypot fields catch lazy bots only

channel: tumblr
kind: value

Honeypot fields catch lazy bots that fill every field, but targeted scripts can skip them. They're good for stopping basic spam, but not enough against form-aware scripts. To strengthen form security, pair honeypots with a server-side check that costs work, like a proof-of-work verification. This approach makes each attempt more expensive for attackers, while honest visitors don't see a thing. On your own site, check if your forms are exposed and how your robots.txt treats AI crawlers.
