# Scan a client's contact form before the first call

channel: discord
kind: promo

Before your first call with a new client, scan their contact form to see which CAPTCHA it shows and which forms are exposed. This simple check can give you a concrete finding to open the conversation about their site's security. The free scanner at https://attestos-edge.proofhand.workers.dev/go/scanner?src=c-promo-agency-scan requires no signup, providing a quick insight into the site's current state. You can discuss server-side verification, key handover, and domain allowlists to improve security and accessibility, while also monitoring verify failures and preparing a spam incident response plan.
