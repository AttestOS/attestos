# A puzzle at checkout taxes the buyer who already decided

channel: tumblr
kind: value

At checkout, a puzzle taxes the buyer who already decided. The person most likely to pay is the one asked to click every traffic light. Who actually sees the puzzle: real buyers on phones, often struggling with small images. What bots do instead: they use solving services or replayed tokens, bypassing the puzzle. Card testing at checkout, credential stuffing on login, fake accounts and promo farming, gift-card guessing - these are the abuses that reach store forms. A visible puzzle puts a cost on real buyers, frustrating those who want to complete their purchase. Moving the check out of sight and onto the server can alleviate this issue, allowing buyers to complete their purchase without unnecessary hurdles.
