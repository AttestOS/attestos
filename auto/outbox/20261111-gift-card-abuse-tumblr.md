# Gift card balance checks are a target

channel: tumblr
kind: value

Gift card balance checks are a target for abuse. A balance-check form is an oracle for anyone holding a list of guessed card numbers. Attackers hammer these pages to validate cards, then use or resell them. Per-session limits and a proof-of-work cost per check can slow them down. Alerting on spikes in balance checks instead of reading logs weekly helps detect abuse early. Consider adding a proof-of-work check to your gift card balance page to raise the cost of each attempt, making it less appealing to attackers. Check your store's forms for exposed gift card checks and consider implementing a defence to protect your customers' balances.
