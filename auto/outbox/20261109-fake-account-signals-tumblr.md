# Fake store accounts leave the same fingerprints

channel: tumblr
kind: value

Fake store accounts often leave behind distinct fingerprints. Mass-created accounts tend to share similar timing, address patterns, and empty profiles. Signs of fake accounts include sign-up bursts at odd hours, the use of disposable email domains, and plus-address runs. Checking the account before it reaches a discount code can help prevent abuse. Other forms of abuse that reach store forms include card testing at checkout, credential stuffing on login, promo farming, and gift-card guessing. Visible puzzles can deter some of this abuse, but they also come at a cost to real buyers. A more subtle approach to verification can help prevent fake accounts without frustrating genuine customers.
