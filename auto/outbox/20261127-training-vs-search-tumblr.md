# Blocking training does not have to block search

channel: tumblr
kind: value

When deciding what AI crawlers can access your content, it's essential to distinguish between different types of crawlers. Search crawlers, like those from major search engines, help your site appear in search results. Training crawlers, on the other hand, collect data to improve AI models. Live fetchers update content in real-time. 

You can set specific robots.txt rules for each type, allowing search crawlers while restricting training ones. Instead of blocking by company, decide by category. Review your rules regularly as new crawlers emerge. Some crawlers verify their identity by published IP ranges, and you can also use HTTP 402 to charge for access or issue agent keys and Private Access Tokens for controlled access.
