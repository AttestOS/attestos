# Scan your store's login and checkout pages

channel: discord
kind: promo

Scan your store's login and checkout pages to see which CAPTCHA they show and which forms are exposed. Abuse like card testing, credential stuffing, and fake accounts can reach your forms. Visible puzzles can frustrate real buyers, raising the cost of each sale. Check your site in minutes with the free scanner: https://attestos-edge.proofhand.workers.dev/go/scanner?src=c-promo-store-scan
