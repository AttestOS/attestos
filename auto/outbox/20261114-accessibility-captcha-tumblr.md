# Image puzzles are an accessibility problem

channel: tumblr
kind: value

Image puzzles block screen reader users and people with low vision first. Audio fallbacks are hard too, as they can be just as inaccessible. Invisible checks need no interaction, making them a more accessible option. When building client sites, it's worth raising accessibility concerns with clients who care. Legal questions around accessibility should be directed to their adviser. Consider server-side verification on every form and key handover with domain allowlists to prevent spam. Monitoring verify failures and having a spam incident response plan in place can also help mitigate issues.
