# Passkeys remove the password attackers reuse

channel: discord
kind: value

Passkeys can help prevent signup and free-trial abuse by removing the risk of reused passwords. A passkey is a unique, per-device authentication credential. Offer passkeys at signup and after login, and keep a recovery path that's not weaker than the passkey. This approach complements proof of work and risk scores, which can be used to flag suspicious activity. By using passkeys and adaptive difficulty, you can make it harder for attackers to abuse your system without frustrating legitimate users.
