Signup and free-trial abuse can be a major issue for SaaS products, with bots exploiting loopholes to obtain API keys or server-side tokens. To combat this, it's essential to implement server-side token verification correctly, using single-use tokens with expiry dates and clear error codes. 

Proof of work can also raise the cost of each attempt for attackers, making it more difficult for them to abuse your system. Risk scores can be used to fork traffic, directing suspicious requests to additional checks. Passkeys can provide an additional layer of security, and adaptive difficulty can help prevent bots from adapting to your defenses.
